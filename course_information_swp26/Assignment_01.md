# Assignment 1

## Part 1: Form the Team

1. Meet your team members and discuss the project idea and objectives.

2. Contact the customer and arrange an introductory meeting as early as possible. **Do not delay this step.**

3. Discuss and assign preliminary team roles and responsibilities.

4. Create and configure your GitLab accounts using the [IU GitLab platform](https://gitlab.pg.innopolis.university).

## Part 2: Prepare the Interview Script and Conduct the Interview

1. **Arrange the Interview:** Contact the customer and schedule a meeting or call. Plan for a 20–30 minute interview, but request a 60-minute time slot if the customer is available. The entire team must attend the interview.

2. **Prepare the Interview Script:** Design an interview script that includes both open-ended and closed-ended questions. The script should help you gain a better understanding of:

   * The project background
   * Business goals and objectives
   * Current workflows and processes
   * Existing challenges and pain points
   * User needs and expectations

3. **Apply the Mom Test Principles:** Improve your interview questions by applying the principles of *The Mom Test*. At the end of the interview script, include notes explaining how the questions were improved. Provide examples of questions before and after the improvements were made.

4. **Recommended Script Creation Process:** Begin by having each team member independently prepare their own list of interview questions. Then combine the individual question lists into a single script and discuss it as a team. Refine, improve, and remove duplicate questions where necessary. Document the key improvements made throughout the process.

5. **Conduct the Interview:**

    * Before the interview, assign roles to team members (e.g., interviewer, moderator, and note taker).
    * Take detailed notes throughout the interview.
    * Record audio or video to ensure that important information is not missed.
    * **Always ask for permission before recording.**
    * Ask follow-up questions when necessary to better understand interesting or important responses.

## Part 3: Research Existing Solutions

1. **Explore Existing Solutions:** Select five existing products, services, or solutions that are similar to the one your customer needs. For each product:

    * Explore the main functionality
    * Identify strengths and weaknesses
    * Capture screenshots of important features
    * Take notes on observations and ideas

2. **Create a Research Board:** Organize your findings in Figma, Miro, or another accessible collaborative tool. The board should include screenshots, notes, key feature observations, and comparisons between the products you analyzed.

3. **Create a Qualitative Analysis Table**: Develop a comparison table that summarizes the key features and characteristics identified across the selected products. The table should help highlight similarities, differences, strengths, and weaknesses.

# Submission Requirements

Submit a single PDF document containing:

1. Title page with team members, assigned roles, and a summary of contributions (who did what during the week).

3. Notes explaining the interview script improvements and application of *The Mom Test* principles. Include examples of questions that were improved.
