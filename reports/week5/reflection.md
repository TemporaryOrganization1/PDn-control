## Reflection
Throughout this project, our team gained valuable experience in both software development and project organization.
One of the most important lessons was documenting the architecture. Creating and maintaining architectural documentation helped us develop a shared understanding of the system, making discussions about design decisions more productive and simplifying future development.
Recording Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) taught us the importance of documenting not only what decisions were made but also why they were made
Refining our development workflow improved collaboration within the team.
Delivering MVP v2 demonstrated the value of iterative development. Building upon the initial MVP allowed us to incorporate feedback, prioritize features, fix issues, and improve the overall quality of the product while keeping development focused on customer needs.
