## Learning points
From customer's feedback, the team learned about the importance of a proper design for future users of the product. Defining quality requirements helped us better understand and think about requirements that are easy to forget about, but very important for the end user. Running UAT helped to undertand the value of real user validation of product functionality. Finally, reviewing increment helped us to better plan changes of the current functionality and plan next features to implement.
