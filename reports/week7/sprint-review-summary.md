## Sprint Goal Reviewed

The Sprint Goal was to implement free/paid plans distribution, integrate a new IP address checking service, fix PDF report formatting issues, and prepare the project for final handover to the customer. The team also needed to demonstrate the functional differences between free and paid tiers.

---

## Delivered Increment Discussed

The team demonstrated the completed freemium model with clear differentiation between free and paid plans:

### Free Plan Features
- Limited check depth (less detailed analysis)
- No PDF report generation
- No website screenshots
- Faster scan completion(due to that less information is provided)
- Basic violation identification only

### Paid Plan Features
- Full functionality with no restrictions
- PDF report download capability
- Website screenshot evidence
- More detailed reports with specific violation locations
- IP address checking (new service integration)
- Complete violation details with addresses

The team also demonstrated:
- Personal account section with plans and tariff management
- Successful plan activation workflow
- PDF report formatting fixes
- New IP checking service integration
- Form validation preventing non-website input
