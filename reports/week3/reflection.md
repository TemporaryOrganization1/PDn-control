## Learning points

The team learned that migrating user stories from a document-based format to an issue-based Product Backlog significantly improved traceability and progress tracking.

Smaller and more clearly defined items after decomposing user stories to smaller PBIs were easier to estimate and implement.

The team learned the importance of selecting a realistic Sprint scope.

The team additionally learned how to use an issue-based workflow

## Validated assumptions

The assumption that all planned MVP v1 functionality could be completed within a single Sprint was partially rejected. PDF report generation required more effort than originally anticipated and was postponed to the next Sprint.

## Friction and gaps

Several backlog items required additional refinement during implementation.

PDF report generation remain incomplete

Additional testing and validation of AI-generated checking responses may be required

## Planned response

In the next Sprint, the team will prioritize the completion of PDF report generation and ensure that all remaining MVP-related functionality is fully integrated and tested.

Large user stories will be decomposed into smaller supporting PBIs earlier in the planning process.

According to the customer's feedback, frontend will be remade to satisfy their expectations.
